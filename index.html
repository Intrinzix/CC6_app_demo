How a donation becomes credits

  1. The donor picks the item type, fabric and condition, and ticks a short self-check. The app shows the credits on offer.
  2. The app issues a QR tag. The donor attaches it and drops the item at North Spine, South Spine or a hall collection point.
  3. A volunteer scans the tag and spot-checks the item against the donor's grade. This takes seconds, so nobody sorts a pile by hand.
  4. Credits are released only after that check. Good items go to the exchange, damaged ones to upcycling, rejects to a partner.

Credits earned per item

Base credits depend on condition. A material factor lowers them for blends and synthetics, which resell poorly and cannot be recycled yet. Results round up, with a minimum of 1.

Guardrails

  • Donation credits are capped at per semester, so donating cannot become a way to fund more buying.
  • Items over the cap are still accepted and diverted, but earn no credits.
  • Cash alternative at the North Spine exchange: 1 credit = S$1, so students with little to donate are not excluded.
  • Volunteers earn credits per hour. These sit outside the donation cap, and no shifts run in exam weeks.
